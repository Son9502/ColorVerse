How to play

Make You match the Target in as few strokes as you can. A stroke is a double-click on any swatch below; each row is a club:

Driver
Complementary: the longest hue jump, straight across the wheel
Wood
Split-comp.: long jumps, just either side of the driver
Hybrids
Triadic, Tetradic, Double-comp.: big jumps around the hue wheel
Iron
Analogous: short hue moves
Wedges
Tints, Shades, Tones, Saturate: lighter, darker, grayer or more vivid, 20–100%. Easy to over- or undershoot. The only clubs out of a bunker; an Expert bunker allows just the one it names
Putter
Small nudges to finish the hole

The meter updates after each stroke. On the green means 2 putts or fewer will hole it.

Par is a good score; perfect (shown after each hole) is the fewest possible. One under par is a birdie. You get 1 free undo per 9 holes, a hole ends at double par, and time breaks ties.

Daily hole: a new hole every day, the same for everyone, following a weekly rotation (Ace Sunday to Championship Saturday). Your first try is your score: once you've played a stroke, leaving counts as a pick-up. Play every day to build a streak.

Practice: endless random holes at the difficulty you choose. Pro: swatches show only the club (e.g. "+120°", "60%"), so you play on color theory alone.

Difficulty: the perfect line under each hole title counts the first shots (of 41) that can still make the perfect score: 6 or more on Beginner, 3–6 on Standard, 1–3 on Expert. Expert also plays as Pro, hides the yardage until you're on the green, and has tighter par (par = perfect on par 3s and 4s, so birdies only come on par 5s).

          How to use ColorVerse

          3-D controls

          Drag or ←↑→↓
          Rotate the object
          Scroll
          Zoom in / out
          [ ]
          Previous / next layer
          1–6
          Snap to a face / axis
          Click
          Pick the color under the cursor
          Double-click
          Reset rotation and zoom
          Esc
          Close this panel, cancel the text box you're in, or go back to Color Search

          Panel tools

          HSL toggle
          Switch the object between RGB and HSL color spaces
          Shape
          Cube or sphere
          Dimension
          3-D now; 1-D and 2-D views are coming soon
          Layer
          Type 0–127 to preview a layer, Enter to jump there. Layer 0 is the outer surface, 127 is the gray core
          Color Search
          Find any of ~3,200 named colors. Enter completes the best match, Enter again selects it. Or pick with ↑↓ + Enter. Click the empty box to see your last 10 searches
          Color details
          Replaces Color Search once a color is selected: hex, RGB, and HSL values, with the active color space bold and listed first. ← Search, Esc, or clicking empty canvas goes back
          Sliders
          Fine-tune the selected color: H/S/L when the HSL toggle is on, R/G/B when it's off. Each track previews what that slider will do
          Harmonies
          Complementary, triadic, analogous, tints, shades, tones, saturate and more. Double-click a swatch to make it the selected color; click ▾ to collapse a row
          Mini shape
          Bottom-right; shows the current orientation and where the picked color sits

          Welcome to ColorVerse

          Most color pickers flatten color into a wheel. But color has three dimensions: RGB is a cube, and HSL is a cylinder.

          ColorVerse lets you rotate those spaces, peel them apart layer by layer, and pick any color to explore its harmonies.
